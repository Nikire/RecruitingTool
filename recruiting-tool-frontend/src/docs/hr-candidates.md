# Candidates

**Where to find it:** Recruitment → Candidates

The Candidates section is your talent database — every person who has ever applied, been added manually, or been evaluated lives here. Think of it as your CRM for people.

---

## The Candidate vs. Applicant Distinction

- A **Candidate** is a person in your database — they have a profile, contact info, and a resume.
- An **Applicant** is a candidate who has applied to a specific job — they have an **Application** tied to a position.

A single candidate can apply to multiple positions. Their profile stays the same; each position gets its own Application and Hiring Process.

---

## What You See in the Candidates List

- **Name and contact info** — email, phone, location
- **Skills and experience** — key tags from their profile
- **Applications** — how many times they've applied and to which positions
- **Status** — active, hired, or rejected in past processes

---

## Adding a Candidate Manually

Click **"Add Candidate"** and fill in:

- **Full name**
- **Email** — used for automated communications
- **Phone** *(optional)*
- **Location** *(optional)*
- **LinkedIn / Portfolio** *(optional)*
- **Skills** — add relevant skill tags
- **Resume/CV** — upload their document (PDF or Word)
- **Notes** — internal notes about this person

Once created, you can create an application for them directly from their profile.

---

## Candidate Profile

Click on a candidate to open their full profile. Here you'll see:

- **Personal info and contact details**
- **Resume** — view or download their attached CV
- **Applications history** — every position they've applied to and their current status
- **Hiring Processes** — all evaluation pipelines they're involved in, past and present
- **Notes** — internal notes added by your team

---

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar to find candidates by name or email. You can also filter by:

- **Skills** — find candidates with specific expertise
- **Status** — active candidates, hired, rejected
- **Application count** — how many times they've applied

---

## Candidate Status Over Time

A candidate's journey in your system:

1. **New** — just added or just applied
2. **In process** — currently in an active Hiring Process
3. **Hired** — was hired for a position
4. **Rejected** — was rejected from one or more processes

A candidate can be rejected for one position and still be in consideration for another.

---

## Tips

- Keep candidate profiles up to date — add notes after interviews or calls
- Don't delete candidates who weren't hired — they may be a great fit for future openings
- Use skill tags consistently so you can filter effectively later
- If a candidate reaches out about a past application, their full history is in their profile
