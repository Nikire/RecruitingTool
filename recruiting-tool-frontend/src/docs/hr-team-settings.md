# Team, Settings & Email Templates

---

## Team Management

**Where to find it:** Settings → Team

If you're an HR Manager or Company Owner, you can manage who has access to your Borderless account and what they can do.

### Roles explained

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **HR** | Review applications, manage candidates, run hiring processes |
| **Recruiter** | Similar to HR — focused on sourcing and applications |
| **HR Manager** | All of the above + manage team members, access AI scoring |
| **Company Admin** | Full access to all HR features and settings |
| **Company Owner** | Everything, including billing and company profile |

### Inviting a team member

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter their email and select their role
4. They'll receive an invitation email to join your workspace

### Removing access

From the Team page, you can deactivate or remove a team member at any time. Their past activity (notes, decisions) stays in the system for reference.

## Email Templates

**Where to find it:** Email Templates (in the sidebar)

Borderless sends automated emails to candidates at key moments in the process. You can customize these templates to match your company's voice.

### Default templates

| Template | When it's sent |
|----------|---------------|
| **Application Received** | When a candidate applies |
| **Application Accepted** | When you accept their application |
| **Application Rejected** | When you reject their application |
| **Hiring Process Access** | Sent with the tracking link and access code |
| **Interview Invitation** | When you schedule an interview |

### Editing a template

Click on any template to edit:
- **Subject line** — the email subject the candidate sees
- **Body** — the full email content
- **Variables** — use placeholders like `{{candidateName}}`, `{{jobTitle}}`, `{{companyName}}` to personalize automatically

> **Tip:** Keep rejection emails warm and professional — candidates remember how companies treat them.

## Company Profile

**Where to find it:** Settings → Company Profile *(Company Owners only)*

Your Company Profile is shown on the public Careers Page when candidates browse open roles. Keep it up to date with:

- **Company name and logo**
- **Industry and company size**
- **Website**
- **Description** — a short pitch about what your company does and why it's a great place to work

A complete company profile builds trust with candidates and improves application quality.

---

## Billing & Subscription

**Where to find it:** Settings → Billing *(Company Owners only)*

From the Billing page you can:

- See your current plan and usage
- View and download invoices
- Upgrade or change your subscription plan

If you're on a trial, this page shows how many days are remaining before you need a paid plan.

---

## Tips

- Customize email templates before you start receiving applications — it sets a professional first impression
- Use variables (`{{candidateName}}`, `{{jobTitle}}`) in templates so every email feels personal even when automated
- Review your team roles periodically — remove access for people who've left the company
- Keep your Company Profile updated — candidates read it before applying
