# Job Positions

**Where to find it:** Recruitment → Job Positions

A Job Position represents an open role at your company. It's the starting point for all hiring activity — candidates apply to positions, and hiring processes are linked to them.

---

## Creating a Job Position

Click **"New Position"** and fill in the details:

- **Title** — The role name candidates will see (e.g. "Senior Frontend Developer")
- **Description** — Full description of the role, responsibilities, and benefits
- **Department / Category** — Helps candidates filter when browsing open roles
- **Job Type** — Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, or Temporary
- **Work Location** — Remote, Hybrid, or On-site
- **Experience Level** — Entry, Mid, Senior, Lead, or Executive
- **Salary Range** — Optional, but helps attract the right applicants
- **Status** — Draft (not visible) or Open (visible on Careers Page)

---

## Setting Up Evaluation Stages

Before saving, define the **stages** candidates will go through during evaluation. These stages become the steps in the Hiring Process when you accept an application.

**Example stages:**
1. HR Screening
2. Technical Test
3. Technical Interview
4. Final Interview
5. Offer

You can add as many stages as you need and reorder them. Each stage can have a name and notes.

> **Important:** The stages you define here become a template. When you accept an application for this position, a personal copy of these stages is created for that candidate's Hiring Process.

---

## Managing Job Positions

From the Job Positions list you can:

- **View details** — See all applications and active hiring processes linked to the position
- **Edit** — Update the description, requirements, or status
- **Close** — Mark the position as closed when the role is filled (stops new applications)
- **Archive** — Remove from active view without deleting

### Position Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Not visible on the Careers Page |
| **Open** | Visible and accepting applications |
| **Closed** | No longer accepting applications |

---

## Job Position Detail Page

Click on any position to open its detail page. Here you'll see:

- **Overview** — Description, requirements, and position info
- **Applications** — All candidates who applied, with their status
- **Hiring Processes** — Active evaluations linked to this position
- **Statistics** — Number of applicants, acceptance rate, time to hire

---

## Tips

- Write a clear, specific job description — it directly affects the quality of applicants
- Set salary ranges when possible — it reduces unqualified applications
- Use stages that match your actual evaluation process — don't add steps you won't use
- Close positions once filled — it keeps your dashboard clean and stops unnecessary applications
