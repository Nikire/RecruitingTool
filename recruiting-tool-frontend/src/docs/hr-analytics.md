# Analytics & Dashboard

---

## Dashboard

**Where to find it:** Dashboard (top of the sidebar)

The Dashboard is your home base — a quick overview of what's happening across your hiring pipeline right now.

### What you see

**Key metrics cards:**
- **Total Applications** — how many applications have come in
- **Active Candidates** — candidates currently in the system
- **Open Job Positions** — roles currently accepting applications
- **Active Hiring Processes** — evaluations currently in progress

**Recent Applications:**
A list of the latest incoming applications so you can act on them quickly. Click any application to open it directly.

**Quick Actions:**
Buttons to jump to common tasks:
- Add a new candidate manually
- Create a new job position
- View all pending applications

> **Tip:** Check your Dashboard at the start of each day. It shows you what's pending and what needs attention.

---

## Analytics

**Where to find it:** Analytics (top of the sidebar)

The Analytics page gives you a deeper view into your hiring performance over time.

### What you can track

**Application volume:**
How many applications came in over a given time period. Helps you understand if your job postings are getting enough visibility.

**Applications by status:**
The breakdown of Pending, Accepted, and Rejected applications — gives you a sense of your selection rate.

**Hiring funnel:**
How candidates move through the stages — where do most candidates drop off? Which stages take the longest?

**Time to hire:**
How many days on average it takes from application to hire. A key metric for understanding your process efficiency.

**Applications by job position:**
Which roles are attracting the most candidates.

### Using the date filter

Most charts let you filter by date range:
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 3 months
- Custom range

Use this to compare performance across different time periods or hiring campaigns.

---

## Tips

- Use Analytics to identify bottlenecks — if candidates are getting stuck at the same stage, it might need a process change
- Track your time-to-hire over time — a lower number means a more efficient process
- If a job position has very few applications, consider updating the description or adjusting the requirements
- Share analytics summaries with leadership to show hiring progress and capacity
