# Interviews & Calendar

**Where to find it:** Scheduling → Interviews / Scheduling → Calendar

Borderless lets you schedule interviews directly inside a Hiring Process and manage them through the company calendar.

---

## Scheduling an Interview

Interviews are scheduled from inside a **Hiring Process**, within a specific stage.

1. Open the Hiring Process for the candidate
2. Go to the current stage
3. Click **Schedule Interview**
4. Fill in the details:
   - **Date and time**
   - **Duration**
   - **Format** — Video call, In-person, Phone
   - **Meeting link** *(optional — paste a Zoom/Google Meet link)*
   - **Interviewers** — team members attending
   - **Notes** — any preparation notes for the interviewer

Once scheduled, the interview appears in:
- The candidate's Hiring Process stage
- The **Interviews list** page
- The **Company Calendar**

---

## Interviews Page

**Where to find it:** Scheduling → Interviews

This page shows all scheduled interviews across all candidates and positions. Use it to:

- See your upcoming interviews at a glance
- Check interview details (time, format, attendees)
- Mark interviews as completed after they happen
- Add outcome notes to feed into the Hiring Process stage

### Filtering interviews

You can filter by:
- **Date range** — today, this week, this month
- **Job Position** — interviews for a specific role
- **Status** — upcoming, completed, cancelled

---

## Company Calendar

**Where to find it:** Scheduling → Calendar

The Company Calendar shows all interviews and meetings in a standard calendar view (day, week, month).

You can:
- See all scheduled events in a visual timeline
- Click on any event to see interview details
- Navigate between days, weeks, and months

### Google Calendar Integration

If your company has connected Google Calendar, interviews scheduled in Borderless are automatically added to your Google Calendar. This means:

- Interviewers receive calendar invites
- Events appear in their personal calendars
- Cancellations in Borderless also remove them from Google Calendar

To connect Google Calendar, go to **Settings → Calendar Settings**.

---

## Calendar Settings

**Where to find it:** Settings → Calendar

From Calendar Settings you can:

- **Connect your Google Calendar** — authorize Borderless to create events on your behalf
- **Set default meeting duration** — so you don't have to fill it in every time
- **Configure working hours** — used when suggesting available time slots

---

## Tips

- Schedule interviews from within the Hiring Process stage, not from the Calendar directly — this keeps everything linked and trackable
- After an interview happens, go back to the stage and add a note with a rating — this keeps the candidate's evaluation record complete
- If you cancel an interview, do it from Borderless so the system stays in sync with your calendar
