# Getting Started with Borderless

Welcome to **Borderless** — your recruiting platform for finding, evaluating, and hiring the right people. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to run your hiring from start to finish.

---

## The Complete Hiring Flow

Every hire in Borderless follows the same path. Understanding this flow is the key to using the platform effectively.

```
Job Position → Application → Hiring Process → Hired ✓
```

Here's what each step means:

### 1. You create a Job Position
A **Job Position** is an open role at your company — for example, *"Frontend Engineer"* or *"Sales Rep"*. You define the title, description, requirements, and set up the evaluation stages (e.g. Screening → Technical Interview → Final Interview).

Once published, the job appears on the public **Careers Page**, where candidates can find and apply for it.

### 2. A Candidate applies → Application
When someone applies through the Careers Page, an **Application** is created. This ties a **Candidate** (the person) to a **Job Position** (the role they want).

You can also add candidates manually if they come through a referral, LinkedIn, or email — and create the application yourself.

From the **Applications** section you review their profile, see their CV, and decide:
- ✅ **Accept** — move them into the hiring process
- ❌ **Reject** — close the application

### 3. You accept → Hiring Process starts
When you **accept** an application, Borderless automatically creates a **Hiring Process** for that candidate. The system copies all the stages you defined on the job position and creates a personal pipeline for this candidate.

The candidate also receives an email with a link to track their own progress.

From here you move them stage by stage — adding notes, scheduling interviews, and using AI scoring to help make your decision.

### 4. Outcome: Hired or Rejected
At any point during the hiring process you can:
- **Mark as Hired** — the candidate is closed as hired, and the job position can be closed
- **Reject** — the process ends, the candidate is notified

## First Steps Checklist

If you're setting up Borderless for the first time:

1. **Set up your Company Profile** — add your company name, logo, and description (Settings → Company Profile)
2. **Create your first Job Position** — define the role and the evaluation stages
3. **Share the Careers Page link** — candidates can apply directly through it
4. **Review incoming Applications** — accept the ones worth evaluating
5. **Run Hiring Processes** — advance candidates through stages, add notes, schedule interviews
6. **Make your decision** — hire or reject, and close the process

> **Tip:** You can also add candidates manually without going through the public job page. Go to **Candidates → Add Candidate** and fill in their info directly.
