# Hiring Processes

**Where to find it:** Recruitment → Hiring Processes

A Hiring Process is the evaluation pipeline for a specific candidate applying to a specific job position. When you **accept an application**, Borderless automatically creates a Hiring Process for that candidate.

This is where the real evaluation work happens.

---

## How It Works

When a Hiring Process is created:

1. The stages you defined on the Job Position are **copied** into this process
2. The **first stage** is set as the current active stage
3. The candidate receives an email with a unique link to **track their own progress**
4. Your team can now work through the stages one by one

---

## The Stages

Stages represent the steps in your evaluation. They were defined when you created the Job Position (e.g. Screening → Technical Test → Final Interview).

### Stage States

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Current** | The stage the candidate is in right now |
| **Done** | This stage was completed and passed |
| **Cancelled** | This stage was skipped |

### What you can do in a stage

**Add a Stage Note**
Write a note about the candidate's performance in this stage. You can also give it a **star rating** (1–5). Notes are visible to your team and are used by the AI scoring system.

**Schedule an Interview**
Link a scheduled interview to the current stage. This creates a calendar event and can send an invite to the candidate.

**Advance to next stage**
When the stage is done, click **Advance** to mark it as Done and move to the next stage.

**Reject**
At any point during the process, you can reject the candidate. This closes the Hiring Process.

---

## AI Scoring

The **AI scoring** feature analyzes a candidate and gives them a **0–100 score** based on how well they match the job position.

The AI looks at:
- Their **full resume/CV** text
- The **job position** description and requirements
- **Stage notes** with ratings your team has added
- **Time spent per stage** (how long they took in each step)

### How to use it

From the Hiring Processes list (grouped view), find the candidate and click **Analyze**. The system will process their information and show a score.

You can re-analyze at any time by clicking **Re-analyze** — for example, after adding more stage notes.

> **Note:** AI scoring is only available to HR Managers and Company Owners.

### Interpreting the Score

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 80–100 | Strong match — highly recommended |
| 60–79 | Good match — worth advancing |
| 40–59 | Partial match — evaluate carefully |
| 0–39 | Weak match — likely not a fit |

The score is a **tool to help your decision**, not a final answer. Always combine it with your team's stage notes and interviews.

---

## Process Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Open** | Just created, evaluation not started |
| **In Progress** | Actively being evaluated |
| **Closed** | Candidate was hired |
| **Rejected** | Candidate was rejected |
| **Cancelled** | Process was cancelled |

---

## Grouped View vs. List View

The Hiring Processes page has two views:

- **Grouped by Job Position** — see all candidates in each position's pipeline side by side. Good for comparing candidates applying to the same role.
- **List view** — shows all processes as a flat list. Good for searching a specific candidate.

---

## What the Candidate Sees

When you accept an application, the candidate receives an email with:
- A link to their **personal tracking page**
- An **access code** to unlock the view

On their tracking page, candidates can see:
- Which stage they're currently in
- Whether they've been accepted or rejected
- General progress through the pipeline

They **cannot** see your internal notes, scores, or team comments.

---

## Ending a Process

**Hiring the candidate:**
Mark the process as **Hired** (Closed). The candidate is now in your hired list. You can then close the Job Position if the role is filled.

**Rejecting the candidate:**
Click **Reject** at any point. The process ends and the candidate can be notified automatically via email template.

---

## Tips

- Add notes after every interaction — even a quick phone screen. These notes feed the AI scoring and help your team remember context weeks later.
- Use stage ratings (1–5 stars) consistently so the AI score reflects real evaluation quality.
- If two candidates are tied, compare their AI scores and stage notes side by side using the grouped view.
- The candidate tracking link is shareable — if a candidate loses their email, you can resend the access code from the process detail.
