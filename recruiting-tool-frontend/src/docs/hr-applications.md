# Applications

**Where to find it:** Recruitment → Applications

An Application is created when a candidate applies to one of your open job positions. This is your main inbox for incoming talent — every new applicant lands here first.

---

## What You See

The Applications list shows you:

- **Candidate name and contact** — name, email, and photo if available
- **Job Position applied for** — which role they're interested in
- **Application date** — when they submitted
- **Status** — Pending, Accepted, or Rejected
- **CV/Resume** — direct access to their attached document

---

## Application Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | New application, not yet reviewed |
| **Accepted** | You accepted them → a Hiring Process was created |
| **Rejected** | You reviewed and decided not to move forward |

---

## Reviewing an Application

Click on any application to open the detail view. You'll see:

- **Candidate profile** — their full information, skills, and experience
- **Cover letter** — if they wrote one
- **CV/Resume** — download or preview their attached document
- **Application notes** — add internal notes visible only to your team

### Making a Decision

Once you've reviewed the application, you have two options:

**Accept ✅**
Clicking Accept does three things automatically:
1. Creates a **Hiring Process** for this candidate and position
2. Copies your evaluation stages into their personal pipeline
3. Sends the candidate an **email with a link** to track their progress

**Reject ❌**
Clicking Reject closes the application. The candidate can be notified automatically via email (based on your email templates).

> **Note:** You can only accept or reject once. If you accept by mistake, the Hiring Process can be cancelled from the Hiring Processes section.

---

## Filtering Applications

Use the search and filters at the top to narrow down the list:

- Search by candidate name or email
- Filter by **Job Position** — see only applicants for a specific role
- Filter by **Status** — show only Pending, Accepted, or Rejected
- Sort by date

---

## Adding Candidates Manually

Not all candidates come through the Careers Page. If a candidate reaches out by email or is referred by someone, you can:

1. Go to **Candidates** and click **Add Candidate**
2. Fill in their info and upload their CV
3. Then create a **manual application** linking them to a job position

This gives them the same flow as self-applied candidates.

---

## Tips

- Review pending applications regularly — candidates appreciate fast responses
- Use application notes to record your first impressions before deciding
- If you're unsure about a candidate, accept them and use the Hiring Process stages to evaluate further — you can always reject later
- Set up email templates to automate rejection emails (Settings → Email Templates)
